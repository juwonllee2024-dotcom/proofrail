# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and direct. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and intentional disruption are not.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
